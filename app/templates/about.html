Our Green Vision

At **GoBharat EV**, we believe the transition to sustainable mobility must be seamless. Standard EV routers calculate routing simply by point-to-point flat distance, ignoring slope angles. This leads to unexpected roadside depletions in regions like the Western Ghats (Lonavala/Khandala climbs).

Our engineering has built a fully custom **topography-aware physical solver**. By calculating rolling resistance, cabin HVAC auxiliary draws, kinetic regenerative recovery, and slope incline vectors, we predict battery depletions with extreme accuracy. In addition, we rank all public charging ports by their live solar renewability scores, letting you charge your vehicle with clean electrons.
