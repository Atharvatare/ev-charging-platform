Data Confidentiality

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026. Your privacy and coordinate security are our highest priority.

01. Data Collection & Profile Information

We collect essential driver information including full name, phone number, email ID, and password during registration. Passwords are securely hashed using state-of-the-art bcrypt-based cryptographic encryption models before persistence. We do not store plaintext credentials.

02. Telemetry Tracking & Location Coordinates

To coordinate optimal routing and charger lockout periods, our map locator monitors geographic bounding boxes and coordinate searches. WebSocket telemetry streams client connection events securely. Location data is analyzed strictly in memory to render charger markers and path lines, and is not sold, shared, or compiled for marketing profiles.

03. Driver Support Tickets Registry

All driver support tickets submitted are saved persistently in the platform's database. When displaying tickets on public dashboard registries (such as the Support Hub queue), driver emails are partially masked (e.g. dri***@ev.com) to prevent scraping, unauthorized exposure, and spam.

04. Global Compliance Standards

GoBharat EV adheres to regional and global privacy protection mandates:

  • GDPR (Europe): European users retain the right to download or permanently delete their profiles and reservations from our SQLite/PostgreSQL ledgers.
  • DPDP Act (India): Strictly requires explicit user authorization before telemetry indexing or SMS notification routing is enabled.
  • CCPA (California): Restricts third-party data tracking and ensures full user visibility into all stored wallet top-ups.
